# WatchParty Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

WatchParty is a browser extension that lets friends watch YouTube videos in sync and chat while watching.

## What the extension uses
To work, WatchParty sends the following to its sync server while you are in a room:
- The display name you choose
- The address (URL) of the page you are currently viewing, so it can tell whether everyone is on the same video
- Playback actions (play, pause, seek, buffering, ad status) and your position in the video
- Chat messages you type into the overlay

## What is stored
- Your display name, a random anonymous ID and your current room code are saved locally in your browser (chrome.storage).
- On the server, rooms exist only in memory. They are deleted a few minutes after everyone leaves, or when the server restarts. Nothing is written to a database.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect your browsing history outside of a room. Your page address is only sent while you are in a room.
- We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising or analytics.
- We do not read or modify video or page content, other than controlling the YouTube video player and showing the overlay.
